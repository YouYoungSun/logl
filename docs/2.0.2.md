# FAULTLINE 2.0.2 — board-first combat presentation

## Scope

This release changes presentation and responsive layout. The board remains ten columns by twenty visible rows, with square cells. Piece generation, rotations, gravity, lock timing, damage, abilities, random attack holes and assisted retry rules remain unchanged.

## Layout

- Desktop gives the board a larger share of the scene, with HOLD/NEXT beside it and the boss and forecast to the right. Canvas size fits the available viewport without stretching individual cells.
- Portrait mobile uses its own composition instead of shrinking the entire desktop scene. Boss HP/core and incoming attack forecast are above the board. The board is centered, with adjacent HOLD/NEXT and compact combo feedback. Touch controls remain reachable and avoid the board.
- Resizing or rotating the screen changes drawing coordinates only. A run is not restarted and the board, queue, seed and ability choices are preserved.

## Combat effects

- Single-line attacks use a sharp, narrow dart. Two- and three-line attacks collect multiple fragments into one projectile. Four-line attacks form a heavier core with four satellite fragments.
- The existing timing contract remains: removal/gathering during the first 60ms, flight until 180ms, then impact feedback. No animation locks, input delays or extra simulation pauses are introduced.
- Attack textures are visible in the gathering phase, while impact geometry and local boss response distinguish attack weight. Damage numbers remain attached to the impact.
- Successful chains create a restrained exterior aura, with bounded stages at one, three and six chains. Protected chains retain the aura. Actual chain breaks end it. Warning colors take priority near the ceiling.
- Reduced effects retains readable states while reducing moving fragments and accents. Effects do not replace the current block, ghost, NEXT or attack warnings.
- Hit sounds use line-count-specific pitch/duration profiles. Four-line hits use a short low-pass texture; increasing combos does not continually increase loudness. Sound-off/pause stops both the note and its texture layer.

## Verification

Run `npm test` and `npm run build`. Rule regression tests are retained, with additional checks for layout geometry, resizing, attack shapes, aura bounds and audio limits. Browser QA covers portrait and desktop play, the final boss forecast and NEXT concealment, reduced effects, menus and the self-contained portable build. Exact artwork and millisecond values were not measured from the reference Shorts; this release adapts its board emphasis and separation of action feedback to FAULTLINE.
