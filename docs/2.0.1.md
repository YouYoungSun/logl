# FAULTLINE 2.0.1

## Cards and player explanations

- Twelve buffs remain. Four-line reattack and end-of-combo charge have been removed, without merging their damage into other abilities.
- Incoming row guard: when the attack countdown shows 01, clear at least two lines with that lock to block one incoming boss row. One/two uses per sector. Existing response reduction happens first; zero incoming rows is allowed. Erosion is not blocked. A lethal clear spends no guard use. Boss attack count, regeneration and phase transitions still advance when all rows are blocked.
- Landing assistance: +75/+150 ms of lock delay. With rigidity, actual delay is `500 + 75 * landing - 35 * rigidity` ms. Gravity, hard drop and the fifteen lock-reset limit are unchanged.
- Reinforcement burden: every third boss attack has one extra row, with the same hole as its attack bundle. One rank only. The attack number and extra row are forecast. Erosion does not advance the attack number. Each sector starts the count again.
- Offer sets include at least one attack and one defense/control buff whenever both pools have eligible candidates. The third slot is unrestricted. Maxed abilities are excluded, including the new one-rank burden. Rerolls use only the reward RNG.
- Card explanations and the guide show conditions, effects, examples and exceptions. Cards identify their attack/defense/control role and actual combined landing delay.

## Combo feedback

- First clear: chain started. Second consecutive clearing lock: 2 COMBO / 2-chain. Clearing two lines at once is not automatically two combos.
- Large fixed counter beside the board updates immediately when lines clear. Damage feedback remains attached to the later projectile impact. Earlier impacts cannot rewind the counter.
- Brief accents at three and six chains. Counts continue beyond six, but the combo damage bonus, sound pitch and effects are capped. The label says the combo bonus is at its maximum, not that the maximum chain is six.
- Grace emits a protected-chain event and keeps the number. A real failed chain emits a quiet ended-chain event. Death, stage transition and restart reset the display without reporting a failed chain.
- No time-limit gauge. No added board shake. Reduced effects keeps readable numbers without pop scaling. Existing clear audio changes pitch rather than stacking a separate combo sound.

## Compatibility and verification

- Rules version and package/UI version are 2.0.1. Standard/assisted records remain separate. Older rule-version records are preserved and the current display identifies its rules version.
- Assisted retry still starts at max(1, death sector - 1), rebuilding exactly start sector - 1 buff/debuff choices.
- Run `npm test` and `npm run build`. Builds include the self-contained portable HTML and existing title art. Tests cover guard/response/erosion/kill ordering, zero-row boss progression, reinforcements, landing timing, eligible roles, combo feedback lifecycle, sound pitch caps and guide/UI flow.
- These are tested rule changes, not measured player win-rate claims. Card selection rates and beginner/expert outcomes still need play testing.
